About Mike Harvey

Mike has called the Waterloo Region home since his early childhood, building a vast social and professional network in the area. His extensive knowledge of the region has been a key factor in his successful real estate career, where he co-founded The Harvey Real Estate Group back in 2011. With over a dozen years of growth and success, Mike is now embarking on a new path to ‘Change’ the way real estate is experienced.

With almost 20 years of experience in the construction industry—both on the ground and in management—Mike has spent considerable time building and renovating homes across the region. This hands-on expertise has shaped his approach to real estate, making him a valuable resource for clients seeking advice on improvement projects, whether large renovations or smaller enhancements.

Known for his hard work, knowledge, and personable nature, Mike earns the trust and respect of his clients. He approaches his real estate practice with honesty and commitment, ensuring that buyers and sellers receive the highest level of service. His ability to connect with clients and understand their goals guarantees a focus on their satisfaction and an attention to detail that sets him apart.
